Resolving wall clock (“local”) readings to instants.

Reading a NaiveDateTime as a wall clock time in an IANA timezone does not always identify a unique instant, because of daylight savings transitions. Everything that has to make that choice – the cast kernel and the string parser – goes through resolve_local_offset so that the two cannot drift apart.
